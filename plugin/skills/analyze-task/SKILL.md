---
name: analyze-task
description: Classify a requested Agent Engine task and identify its risk before planning.
---

Recover owner/project context. Classify the highest applicable risk: Quick for localized reversible work; Standard for related components; Deep for security, persistence, money, architecture or migrations. State a concise objective, affected and reused components, acceptance criteria and verification. List important findings or None. Never lower risk merely because a diff is small. Register the task through authenticated Agent Engine MCP; do not fabricate operational state.
