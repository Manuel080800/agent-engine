Run coordinator verify for the registered tests. A summary cannot replace typed LOCAL_CHECKED evidence. Compare the full managed changes against the original plan, every acceptance criterion and required rule. Report findings with a stable identity, requirement, relative file, signature and blocking/warning severity. Corrections are limited to two per finding, three per block, six per task and two final rounds. Reverify after a correction. Before release, perform final review across blocks; pending criteria or blocking findings prohibit completion. Server evidence validation checks contracts and provenance channels, not semantic correctness of code.
